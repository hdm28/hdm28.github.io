Reverse Engineering

REVERSE ENGINEERING | YEAR 4 IDE NOVEMBER 2022 | 4 WEEKS
Analysis Disassembly Electronics Documentation

Systematic reverse engineering analysis of an interactive dance mat, involving complete disassembly, component identification, and documentation of manufacturing processes and design decisions. The project demonstrated methodical analysis techniques and engineering documentation skills.

The study included layer-by-layer analysis, electrical circuit mapping, material identification, and assessment of manufacturing methods. The project provided insights into commercial product design and cost-optimization strategies.
