Reverse Engineering

AUTUMN 2022

I was given a kids interactive dance mat to analyse and disassemble in order to produce 3 sets of infographics:

  • User experience
  • Component role and function
  • Manufacturing and cost
Every two weeks, I submitted 2x A3 posters focusing on different aspects of the product, gaining more insight as a I disassembled and analysed different sub-systems in depth.

This project developed both graphic design and analytical thinking skills, requiring me to understand not just how the product functioned, but why specific design decisions were made throughout the development process.
