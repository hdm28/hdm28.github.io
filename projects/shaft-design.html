Comprehensive mechanical design project involving shaft assembly design, stress analysis, and detailed engineering documentation. The project required calculation of loads, selection of appropriate materials, and design of supporting structures and bearing assemblies.

The design process included fatigue analysis, safety factor calculations, and optimization for manufacturing cost and performance. Detailed technical drawings and specifications were produced following industry standards.
