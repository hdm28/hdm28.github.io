Gearbox Design

MECHANICAL DESIGN | YEAR 2 IDE MAY 2020 | 4 WEEKS
Mechanical Design Hand Drawing Gear Design Technical Drawing

Comprehensive gearbox design project featuring detailed hand-drawn technical drawings and mechanical calculations. The project demonstrated traditional engineering drawing skills alongside modern design principles for gear train optimization and mechanical power transmission.

The design included gear ratio calculations, torque analysis, bearing selection, and housing design. All technical drawings were completed by hand to develop fundamental drafting skills and attention to detail in engineering documentation.
