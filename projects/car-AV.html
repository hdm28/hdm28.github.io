Car Sensor System

2025

Design and implementation of a performance data acquisition and audio system for classic car applications. The system integrates modern sensor technology with vintage automotive systems to provide real-time performance monitoring and enhanced audio capabilities.

The project features Arduino-based data logging, temperature monitoring, audio system integration, and custom dashboard displays designed to complement the classic car aesthetic while providing modern functionality.
