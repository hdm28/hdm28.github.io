Pick-and-Place Balloon Gripper

MECHANICAL ENGINEERING | YEAR 4 IDE JANUARY 2023 | 3 WEEKS
Soft Robotics Pneumatics CNC Design

Design and build a soft robotic gripper system for a CNC pick-and-place application. The project involved developing an inflatable balloon gripper that could safely handle delicate objects while being precise enough for automated manufacturing tasks.

The system uses pneumatic control to inflate and deflate soft balloon grippers, allowing them to conform to objects of various shapes and sizes. The gripper was integrated with a custom gantry system for precise positioning and movement control.
