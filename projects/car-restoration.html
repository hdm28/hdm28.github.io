MG Midget Restoration

AUTOMOTIVE RESTORATION | PERSONAL 2020-2021 | 18 MONTHS
Mechanical Engineering Restoration Engine Rebuild Electrical Systems

Complete restoration of a 1974 MG Midget, including engine rebuild, electrical system overhaul, bodywork restoration, and mechanical component refurbishment. The project combined traditional automotive engineering with modern restoration techniques.

The restoration involved dismantling the entire vehicle, rebuilding the A-Series engine, rewiring the electrical system, treating rust, repainting bodywork, and reassembling all mechanical systems to factory specifications and beyond.
