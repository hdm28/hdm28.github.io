Rolling Robot

MECHATRONICS PROJECT | YEAR 4 IDE MARCH 2023 | 8 WEEKS
Mechatronics Control Systems Arduino Robotics

Design and construction of a hamster ball mechatronics robot capable of autonomous navigation and remote control. The project combined mechanical design, electronic control systems, and programming to create a unique rolling locomotion system.

The robot features gyroscopic stabilization, wireless control capabilities, and sensor integration for obstacle avoidance. The spherical design presented unique challenges in power distribution, sensor mounting, and motion control algorithms.
