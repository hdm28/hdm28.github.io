Wind Turbine Optimization

AERODYNAMICS OPTIMIZATION | YEAR 4 IDE OCTOBER 2023 | 6 WEEKS
CFD Analysis Optimization Aerodynamics MATLAB

Computational optimization study of wind turbine blade design using genetic algorithms and computational fluid dynamics. The project focused on maximizing energy extraction efficiency while minimizing material usage and manufacturing complexity.

Using MATLAB optimization toolbox and CFD analysis, multiple blade geometries were evaluated to determine optimal chord length, twist angle, and airfoil selection for various wind conditions and operational requirements.
